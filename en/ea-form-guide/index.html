EA forms in 5 steps

Every employer must give each employee an EA form (Form C.P.8A) showing their income and deductions for the year. Here's how to get it right.

  1. Close the year's payroll

    Finalise December's payroll so every month of the year is complete.

  2. Total each employee's income

    Salary, overtime, commission, bonus, allowances, benefits in kind and accommodation, each in its own line.

  3. Total deductions and contributions

    PCB and any CP38 deductions, zakat, and the employee's EPF and SOCSO contributions.

  4. Fill in the official form

    Use the EA form from LHDN's website. Check names, IC or passport numbers and income tax numbers.

  5. Hand it out and file Form E

    Give every employee their EA form, including anyone who left during the year, then submit Form E with the C.P.8D list.

Key dates

EA forms to employeesBy end of February
Form E to LHDNBy 31 March
Form E by e-FilingUsually by 30 April

Common mistakes

  • Leaving out benefits in kind
  • PCB totals that don't match the monthly CP39 payments
  • Forgetting staff who left during the year
  • Wrong IC or income tax numbers

General guidance only. Check the latest requirements on LHDN's website or with your tax agent.

What goes into an EA form

  • Employee details: name, IC or passport number, income tax number, EPF and SOCSO numbers.
  • Employment income: salary, overtime, commission, bonus, allowances, benefits in kind and accommodation.
  • Deductions: PCB, any CP38 instalments and zakat paid through payroll.
  • Contributions: the employee's share of EPF and SOCSO.
