How this PCB estimate is worked out

PCB (Potongan Cukai Bulanan) is the monthly tax employers deduct from salaries and pay to LHDN by the 15th of the following month.

The calculator projects the year's chargeable income from the monthly salary, subtracts individual relief of RM9,000, spouse relief of RM4,000 if your spouse doesn't work, RM2,000 for each child, and EPF relief up to RM4,000, then spreads the year's tax over 12 months.

EPF is 11% for the employee and 13% for the employer on wages up to RM5,000 (12% above that). Foreign workers contribute 2% each. SOCSO and EIS are worked out on wages up to RM6,000.
